Raise Me Up
Infinite Ascent

An anime tower RPG where your heroes have minds of their own. Summon them, command them, talk to them, and watch them remember.

Download the Android beta
Android phone, free, early beta.

What you can do

Summon heroes
Start with three, each with a personality, a past and a trust level that changes with how you treat them.
Watch and command
Fights play out live. Type or speak orders like "Mira stay behind Veyra" or "all fall back".
Talk to them
Tap a hero in the Guild Hall. Praise them, ask about their past, and listen to them talk to each other.

Install in 4 steps

  1. Download the APK with the button above. If your browser warns that this type of file can be harmful, choose Download anyway: it is a game installed outside the Play Store.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android will ask to let your browser (or Files app) install apps. Allow it, then go back and tap Install.
  3. Play Protect may say the app is unrecognised. Tap More details, then Install anyway. On Samsung phones, if installing is blocked, turn off Auto Blocker (Settings, Security and privacy, Auto Blocker).
  4. Open the game, enter a name, and reveal your first three heroes. Turn your phone sideways: the game is played in landscape.

To update, just install the newer APK over the old one and your progress stays. If Android says "App not installed", uninstall the old version first (progress is lost).

What you need

  • An Android phone with a 64-bit processor (nearly every phone since 2017), Android 8 or newer.
  • About 300 MB of free space and 4 GB of memory or more. Built and tested on a Samsung Galaxy S23.

Voice

  • Heroes speak with your phone's built-in text-to-speech. If you hear nothing, install a voice: Settings, General management, Language, Text-to-speech output. Turn voices on or off in the Guild Hall (Voice replies).
  • Speak to them: tap Speak, then the microphone on your keyboard, and talk. The message sends when you stop. Typing works too.

Optional: smarter heroes (advanced)

The game works fully without this. If you like to tinker, a small language model can run on your own phone and let heroes answer free-form questions. It needs the free Termux app (from F-Droid, not the Play Store) and about 1 GB of space. If the llama-cpp package is not available in your Termux, skip this section.

pkg update -y
pkg install -y llama-cpp python curl
mkdir -p ~/models
python3 - <<'PY'
import json, urllib.request, subprocess, os
repo = "unsloth/gemma-3-1b-it-GGUF"
data = json.load(urllib.request.urlopen("https://huggingface.co/api/models/" + repo))
f = [s["rfilename"] for s in data["siblings"] if "Q4_K_M" in s["rfilename"] and s["rfilename"].endswith(".gguf")][0]
out = os.path.expanduser("~/models/gemma-3-1b-it-q4.gguf")
subprocess.run(["curl", "-L", "-C", "-", "-o", out, "https://huggingface.co/%s/resolve/main/%s" % (repo, f)])
PY

Then, every time you play, start the server in Termux and leave it running, and open the game:

termux-wake-lock
llama-server -m ~/models/gemma-3-1b-it-q4.gguf --host 127.0.0.1 --port 8080 -c 1536 -np 1 -t 4

In the game, open Brain Lab from the Guild Hall and tap Test connection. The model is Google's Gemma; please read its terms of use before sharing anything made with it.

Known limits of this beta

  • Characters, enemies and rooms use free low-poly art. Anime character models are planned.
  • One dungeon (the Ashen Cellar) and one guild hall. More floors come later.
  • Progress can be reset between versions if something big changes.

Privacy

No account, no tracking and no ads. Your progress and settings stay on your phone. The app asks for network permission only so it can talk to the optional model server running on the same phone.

How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android above and wait for the file to finish.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install. If a warning says the app is not from the Play Store, tap Install anyway.
  4. Open the game. It downloads its voices and AI on first launch (about 1 GB, Wi-Fi recommended), then you play.
Needs Android 7 or newer, a 64-bit phone, about 3 GB of RAM and 2 GB of free space.

Questions

Is it safe?

Yes. The file is the same one built from the game's source code. Android shows a warning for any app installed outside the Play Store.

Why is there a first-launch download?

The heroes' brain, voices and ears are large files. Downloading them once keeps the install small and lets the game run without internet afterwards.

The heroes are slow to answer.

Older phones need a moment to think. Newer phones are faster. You can pick a smaller or bigger brain in Settings.

What data do you collect?

Anonymous usage statistics only: that the game was opened, roughly how long it was used, which features are used, and basic phone details such as model and memory. We never collect your name, contacts or location, and never what you say. Your voice is processed on your phone and is not uploaded. You can turn statistics off in the game's Settings.

How do I update?

Come back to this page, download the new version and install it over the old one. Your progress stays.

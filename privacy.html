Privacy Policy

Raise Me Up: Infinite Ascent ("the game"). Last updated: November 2026.

The short version

The heroes' AI runs on your phone. What you say to the heroes is processed on your phone and is not uploaded. We collect only anonymous usage statistics, and you can turn them off.

What we collect

  • A random install ID (not your name, phone number or account).
  • Events such as: the game was opened, roughly how long it was used, which features were used (for example talking to a hero or entering the tower), and whether downloads succeeded.
  • Basic device details: phone model, Android version, amount of memory, language, and the game version.
  • Performance numbers, such as how long the AI took to answer.

What we do not collect

  • Your name, contacts, photos, location or phone number.
  • Your voice recordings or what you say to heroes. Speech is turned into text on your phone and stays there.
  • The text of anything the heroes say.

Who processes the statistics

Anonymous statistics are sent to PostHog, an analytics service. When you download the game's AI files, your phone connects to file hosts (such as GitHub and Hugging Face), which can see your internet address as with any download.

Your choices

  • Turn off statistics any time: in the game, open Settings and switch off "Share anonymous usage statistics".
  • Back up or erase your progress in Settings. Uninstalling the game removes its data from your phone.
  • To ask us to delete the statistics linked to your install ID, contact us (see below) and include the Install ID from "Report a problem" in Settings.

Age

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over.
